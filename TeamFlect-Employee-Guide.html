Welcome to TeamFlect

TeamFlect is the system ITDP uses for goals, one-on-one meetings, and annual performance reviews. It also includes surveys and recognitions, but ITDP does not use those, so this guide does not cover them.

What we use TeamFlect for

🎯 Goals

Set the outcomes you are working toward and track progress against them.

📝 Annual performance

Complete your self-review when your review cycle opens, and set your goals for the next year.

🗣️ 1:1s

Keep a shared, running agenda with your manager between formal reviews.

Section 2

Getting started

You can use TeamFlect in Microsoft Teams, in Outlook, and in a web browser.

Getting started

TeamFlect uses single sign-on with your Microsoft work account. You do not create a separate username or password. Choose one of the two options below.

🌐 Option 1: Web browser

  1. 1
    Open your browser and go to app.teamflect.com.
  2. 2
    Select Sign in with Microsoft.
  3. 3
    Use the username and password you use for Microsoft Teams.

💬 Option 2: Microsoft Teams

  1. 1
    Open Microsoft Teams, select Apps in the left rail, and search for TeamFlect.
  2. 2
    Select Add, then sign in with your Microsoft work account and password.
  3. 3
    Pin TeamFlect to your Teams sidebar for one-click access.
🔔
Notifications come through Teams

You get a Teams notification when a review cycle opens. Complete your self-assessment by the deadline in that notification.

🔐
Can't sign in?

The sign-in page links to a help article for login trouble. If that doesn't fix it, contact your TeamFlect administrator.

Use TeamFlect on mobile

📲 Teams mobile app

Install the Microsoft Teams mobile app, then add Teamflect to your navigation bar.

🌍 Mobile browser

Open app.teamflect.com and sign in. No download is needed.

Goals: High-Level Overview

How ITDP uses Goals in TeamFlect

ITDP uses TeamFlect to track and assign goals. Goals are set at two levels: Individual SMART goals and Team goals. Individual goals support a team key result or your role, so the work you track in TeamFlect shows how your goals support the team.

You also use TeamFlect to reflect on your goals for the current year. Those reflections feed into your annual review, so update your progress throughout the year rather than at the end.

Two kinds of goals

Individual SMART goals

  • Achieved through your own effort.
  • What you deliver and how you work.
  • Support a team key result or your role.

Team goals

  • Achieved by collective effort.
  • Results the team works to influence.
  • Each key result has an owner, who guides it but does not own the result alone.
Find the Goals module
  1. 1
    Open TeamFlect in Microsoft Teams or your web browser.
  2. 2
    Select Goals from the left-hand menu.
  3. 3
    Stay on the My goals tab to view and manage your individual goals.
How to create a goal in TeamFlect
  1. 1
    Select New goal at the top of the page.
  2. 2
    Enter a title for your goal.
  3. 3
    Add a description that explains what you want to achieve and how you will measure success.
  4. 4
    Complete the goal fields listed below. Your organization's settings control which fields appear.
  5. 5
    Choose how you will measure progress, then enter the starting value and the target.
  6. 6
    Review the goal and select Create.
Common questions

Can I create a goal for someone else? Managers and other authorized users may be able to create or assign goals, depending on permissions.

Can I edit my goal after I create it? Yes, if you have edit permissions. Open the goal and update its details or progress.

Reviews

What is performance management?

Performance management is an ongoing process where you and your manager set clear expectations, share feedback and support, recognize your achievements, and identify opportunities for improvement to help you succeed and contribute to the advancement of organizational goals.

Built in steps
  1. 1
    Current year: Evaluate. A fair rating for everyone, from different starting points: current-year goals or your role description.
  2. 2
    Next year: Set clear goals. Consistent use of team goals and individual SMART goals, with a mid-year check-in.
  3. 3
    Longer term: Build the foundation. Job levels, salary bands, and career paths.
Annual Review Cycle

An annual performance review is a comprehensive assessment of your performance, completed once a year. TeamFlect runs the full review cycle in one place, from your self-review through to finalization.

  • Holistic view: The annual review gives a full picture of your performance across the year.
  • Connected to your development: The review links to your self-development plan, so improvement steps and goals carry forward.

Your annual review looks at your current-year results against your goals, and it is where you set your next-year goals. Complete the steps below in order. Your manager and HR handle the steps after your self-review.

Navigating to your review in TeamFlect
Complete your self-review
  1. 1
    Open the review. In Teams, open the TeamFlect chat and select See review on the New review card. You can also select Reviews from the TeamFlect left menu.
  2. 2
    On the Reviews About Me tab, find your review under Waiting for signature and select Sign review.
  3. 3
    Read the review header. It lists the reviewer, the review period, the date your self-review was submitted, the review due date, and the finalize date.
  4. 4
    Work through each section. Your answers appear beside your reviewer's answers for each question, and each rating question shows both ratings.
  5. 5
    The review stays locked until you sign it. Select Sign now to add your signature.
Rating scale for each goal
LabelWhat it means
Outstanding ContributionConsistently performs above expectations, with impact beyond your role, sustained over the year.
Successful PerformanceConsistently meets expectations and delivers on your responsibilities.
Partially Meets ExpectationsMeets some, but not all, expectations. Clear areas to improve.
Unsatisfactory PerformanceGenerally applies to staff on a performance improvement plan or with a formal written warning.

Meetings and 1:1s

Manager-employee 1-on-1 meetings are essential for building strong relationships, providing feedback, and setting clear objectives. TeamFlect offers a dedicated space for these interactions.

Your 1:1 page is an agenda that you and your manager share. You can open it three ways: in TeamFlect, from the Teams calendar invite, or from the TeamFlect panel during the meeting. All three take you to the same page.

How to get there
  1. 1
    Open the 1:1 from the Meetings menu, or join from the Teams calendar invite.
  2. 2
    Add your talking points before the meeting. Your manager's items appear on the same page.
  3. 3
    Pull an open goal into the conversation for a live status check instead of updating it separately.
  4. 4
    Assign action items to either of you as they come up.

Set how often you meet under My Settings. Your administrator sets the maximum frequency for your company.

Monthly team check-in

A monthly team check-in is a meeting where your team reviews progress, aligns on goals, discusses challenges, and plans the month ahead.

  1. 1
    Schedule the meeting in TeamFlect and invite your team members.
  2. 2
    Create an agenda with talking points, goals, and action items.
  3. 3
    During the meeting, use TeamFlect's integrated features for real-time collaboration.
  4. 4
    Share notes, attachments, and resources.
  5. 5
    Track task and goal progress during the meeting.
  6. 6
    Summarize the key takeaways in the meeting notes and assign follow-up tasks.

Alignment

Team members stay aligned on goals and priorities.

Efficiency

Collaborative meeting tools help the team get more done.

Accountability

Meeting notes and action items keep everyone accountable.

Using TeamFlect for 1:1s

A 1:1 tool streamlines your process and builds useful insight over time. TeamFlect keeps your 1:1 agenda, progress, and goals in one place, and you can open it inside a Microsoft Teams meeting.

In TeamFlect, you can:

  • Schedule and get reminders for your 1:1s automatically.
  • Build agendas from templates with recurring talking points and check-in questions.
  • Track progress over time so you can see how your work is moving between meetings.
  • Bring goals into your Teams meetings to review them live.
  • Connect your 1:1 notes to your performance review, since the same platform runs the review cycle.
Open TeamFlect in a Teams meeting
  1. 1
    In your Teams meeting, find Apps in the meeting interface, search for Teamflect, and select it to add.
  2. 2
    Select the Teamflect icon in the meeting controls. It opens in a panel on the right.
  3. 3
    In the panel, view the agenda and talking points, take shared or private notes, add tasks, and present your goals and reviews.
